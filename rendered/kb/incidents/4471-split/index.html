Two defects, two engineering issues

  • Shared parser: issue #1. The shared parser reads month first for day-first tenants. For example, R-41200 (harbour.dental, app) says a 7 April reminder went out on 4 July. Reproduce with 07/04/2026: the tenant means 7 April, but month-first parsing yields 4 July. Fix and test an explicit tenant date contract across shared-parser paths.
  • Booking-v3 hint: issue #2. Booking-v3 displays MM/DD/YYYY, prompting month-first input, while the form interprets it in the tenant’s day-first order. R-41230 (copper.studio, form) reports 1 June instead of 6 January. Entering 01/06/2026 following that hint reproduces the mismatch. Align the displayed hint and submission contract; test both, separately from the parser fix.

Uncertainty stays visible

The desk-noted reports above describe one-day, calendar-boundary or delivery-timing symptoms, not demonstrated month/day transpositions. Keep them within their current parser assignment pending investigation. They do not prove a timezone/DST defect, nor a third root cause; do not discard them or count them again. Other assignments also need case-level confirmation. Locale and channel explain the model’s grouping, not a verified reproduction for every report.

Proposed desk workflow

  1. Keep one report per ID. Read the body, intended date, resulting date and channel; look up the tenant’s locale and booking form. Do not infer the contract from browser locale or meta.intakeTag.
  2. Compare reproduction paths and causes before merging. Route shared-parser evidence to issue #1 and booking-v3 hint evidence to issue #2, keeping the original report IDs and a cross-reference to #4471. A matching subject is a search aid, not a merge decision; different subjects can still share a cause.
  3. If evidence is insufficient, mark the assignment provisional and ask for the input, expected/output dates and path. Investigate the desk-noted cases without inventing a third bucket or inflating counts.
  4. Ask the support desk to approve a new decision replacing the subject-only rule before adopting this as policy. Preserve accepted decision 0001 as history. Verify each defect independently before claiming #4471 resolved.
